# Changelog

## v1.1.0 — 2026-10-12

- **Windows version** (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit), with an installer and
  built-in updates, like the Mac version.
- **Appearance** setting in Preferences: Dark (default), Light, or Same as
  System.
- Leaving the full-screen preview restores a maximized window.
- Menu fixes: Compare shows its real key (`:`), Guide shows F1.

## v1.0.0 — 2026-10-07

First public release.

Trichr-o-matic recomposes a color image from three black & white photos shot
through red, green and blue filters (or infrared, Aerochrome-style and custom
filter sets). It is also a complete editor for single photos.

- **Three modes**: Classic Trichrome (3 B&W shots), Color Trichrome (3 color
  shots, one channel each, for a "Harris Shutter" look) and Solo (one photo).
- **Batch Import** that matches R/G/B triplets by filename, by sequence or by
  hand, with optional automatic alignment.
- **Per-channel alignment and correction**: automatic alignment, position,
  lens distortion, perspective, local stretch and tone, channel by channel.
- **Editing**: Light, Color, Black & White, Negative, Curves, Histogram, and
  Framing (crop, straighten, mirror, guided perspective).
- Camera **RAW** support, 16-bit TIFF export, portable `.trirgb` session
  files, undo/redo, a customizable block layout, Light mode, an HQ Preview,
  English and French interfaces, a guided Quick Tour, and an update check.
- **Scan** (experimental, off by default): tethered capture from a camera
  with `gphoto2`. Turn it on in Preferences ▸ Experimental.
